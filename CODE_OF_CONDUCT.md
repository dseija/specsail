# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the SpecSail community a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include being considerate, communicating constructively, accepting feedback gracefully, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, or publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through the contact options listed on the repository. Reports will be reviewed and handled respectfully and confidentially.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently exclude contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
